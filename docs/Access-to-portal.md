# How to access the Portal 

## Portal environments:
- Dev : https://check-performance-data-development.test.teacherservices.cloud/
- QA : https://check-performance-data-qa.test.teacherservices.cloud/
- Preprod : https://preproduction.check-performance-data.education.gov.uk/
- Prod : https://check-performance-data.education.gov.uk/

## DfE SignIn Account setup

### DEV and QA

- Create an account on Dfe SignIn's **TEST** environment here: https://test-services.signin.education.gov.uk/
- Request access to a school.  Current schools with seeded data are:
  - Kingsmead School (860/4070) - *Included and Non-Included data*
  - Minehead Middle School (933/4290) - *Included and Non-Included data*
  - Eastbrook School (301/4023) - *Non-Included data only*
  - Alderwood School (850/2729) - *Included data only*
- **WAIT** for an email from DfE SignIn to confirm you have been granted access to the school.  This can take about 24 hours.
- Let a member of the CYPMD Development team know that your account has been granted access to the school and they will add you to the CYPMD Service.
