## DfE SignIn Account setup

### DEV and QA

- Create an account on Dfe SignIn's **TEST** environment here: https://test-services.signin.education.gov.uk/
- Request access to a school.  Current schools with seeded data are:
  - Kingsmead School (860/4070) - *Included and Non-Included data*
  - Minehead Middle School (933/4290) - *Included and Non-Included data*
  - Eastbrook School (301/4023) - *Non-Included data only*
  - Alderwood School (850/2729) - *Included data only*
- Once you've been granted access to the school (can take about 24 hours and is carried out by the DfE SignIn team), let a member of the dev team know who will grant you access to the service.


### Preprod

For **Preprod**, you'll need an account on Dfe SignIn's **PREPROD** environment here: https://pp-services.signin.education.gov.uk/

### Prod

For **Prod**, you'll need an account on Dfe SignIn's **PROD** environment here: https://services.signin.education.gov.uk/
